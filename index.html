Registlet List

The level inside the bracket means the possible drop from corresponding Registlet dungeon level.

Rarity may be inaccurate. It is divided into:

  • Common: 60% Chance
  • Uncommon: 25% Chance
  • Rare: 10% Chance
  • Super Rare: 4.25% Chance
  • Epic: 0.65% Chance
  • Legendary: 0.1% Chance
